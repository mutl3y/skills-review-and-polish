# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone who participates in this project and its community will be treated with respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Be respectful** — Treat all community members with kindness and respect
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- **Listen actively** — Value different perspectives and experiences
- **Assume good intent** — Interpret others' statements charitably
- **Provide constructive feedback** — Focus on ideas, not people
- **Respect privacy** — Don't share others' personal information without consent

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or other protected status
- Threats or intimidation
- Publishing others' private information ("doxxing")
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Community members who observe unacceptable behavior should report it by contacting the project maintainers at:

- **Email**: [maintainers contact — to be determined]
- **GitHub Issue**: Report with "Code of Conduct Violation" title (private)
- **Anonymous**: [Contact mechanism — to be determined]

### Response Process

1. We will investigate all reports promptly and confidentially
2. We will take action proportional to the severity of the violation
3. Actions may include:
   - Private conversation to clarify expectations
   - Public apology if appropriate
   - Temporary suspension from community spaces
   - Permanent ban from the project (serious cases)

4. We will follow up with the reporter about the outcome (unless they prefer not to know)

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions in this repository
- Project-related communication channels (Discord, Slack, forums, etc.)
- Project events and conferences
- Interactions between community members related to this project

Behavior outside the project scope that violates this Code of Conduct may result in enforcement if it affects community safety or project integrity.
